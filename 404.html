Our Mission

Four values guide every project we run.

  • Inclusion & Integration

    Opening local and European mobility projects to IDPs, migrants and young people with fewer opportunities.

  • Active Citizenship

    Involving young people in international educational programmes that support Georgia’s European integration.

  • Tolerance & Diversity

    Fostering respect and equality towards diverse cultures, values and ways of life.

  • Environment

    Drawing attention to local environmental problems and turning them into youth-led action.

Georgian Youth Development Association was founded to make active citizenship accessible, engaging and practical.

We empower young people through non-formal education to develop interpersonal skills and social competences. Our goal is to foster European values, gender equality, and successful integration into the international community.

Our Background and Community Roots

The Georgian Youth Development Association (GYDA) is a non-governmental, non-profit youth organization based in the Gldani district of Tbilisi, an area with a historically significant population of Internally Displaced Persons from Abkhazia and South Ossetia. For nearly a decade, our organization has dedicated itself to supporting these young individuals, ensuring their seamless integration into society through access to free educational resources and European mobility projects. We work with a modern vision, adapting internationally certified methodologies to foster the interpersonal and social competences of our local youth while actively championing Georgia’s integration into the European Union.

Our impact is driven by hands-on community

Civic Engagement and Strategic Partnerships

Our impact is driven by hands-on community engagement and strategic institutional partnerships. We recently signed a memorandum with Grigol Robakidze University—making us only the second organization after DRONI to secure such an agreement—granting us access to university premises for diverse non-formal workshops and gatherings. We have also hosted significant diplomatic and civic events, including an interactive lecture in Bakuriani with Mr. Andrius Kalindra, the Ambassador of the Republic of Lithuania, focusing on the EU’s role in the South Caucasus and the importance of peaceful conflict resolution. Furthermore, our "Pieces of Peace" Karl Popper-style debate tournaments at the University of Georgia and the historic Peace Walk Georgia at the Mikheil Saakashvili Presidential Library have successfully united youths, politicians, and local citizens to advocate for international peace agreements.

Grassroots Charity and Environmental Action

Beyond civic education, GYDA is deeply committed to grassroots charity and environmental stewardship. We successfully collaborated with the "Merrily Masks" theatre to raise over one thousand GEL for stage decorations and costumes, an initiative that was featured in a short reportage by the Georgian Public Broadcaster, Pirveli Arkhi. Our team has also directly supported young people with disabilities, successfully collecting funds to provide new wheelchairs for local youth named Luka and Mariam. On the environmental front, our "Let’s keep our city Clean" campaign brought together members and volunteers to clear over five thousand square meters of territory around Tbilisi. Through meticulous planning, administrative management, and comprehensive monitoring of Erasmus+ and Youth in Action programs, our dedicated team continues to create meaningful, lasting impacts across the region.

GYDA projects

Capacity building in the field of youth

  • Erasmus+ CBY · 2024–2027SYWOR

    Strengthening Youth Workers and Organisations for Displaced Youth Integration and Resilience

    Project no. 101185795

    “Stronger Together” supports displaced youth, both refugees and internally displaced persons, in Ukraine, Georgia, Turkey, Moldova and Poland. It equips youth workers and their organisations with tools and skills such as trauma-informed care and cross-cultural communication, helping displaced young people integrate, build resilience and take an active part in their communities.

    Learn more
  • Erasmus+ CBYPACT

    Psychological First Aid and Creative Techniques

    Project no. 101186111

    PACT strengthens youth mental health support with a methodology that combines psychoeducation, psychological first aid and art therapy. It trains youth workers, promotes early intervention and resilience for vulnerable groups, develops e-learning tools and works to reduce stigma by bringing mental health education into youth programmes across partner countries.

    Learn more
  • EU GRANewly funded · Kick-off Dec 2026
    Erasmus+ CBY · Coordinated by GYDAEU GRA

    Generation of Resilient Allies

    Project no. 101329208

    EU GRA helps youth organisations in the EU and the Eastern Partnership counter disinformation and build civic resilience. Its Train-the-Trainer programme in media literacy and fact-checking will train 120 youth workers, reach 600 young people, including refugees and IDPs, and produce an open toolkit. Partners: France, Czechia, Türkiye, Armenia and Ukraine.

Youth Exchanges

  • Youth Exchange

    Digital Dance

    Gonio, Georgia

    The project explored the connection between dance, digital creativity, and intercultural communication. Through workshops, creative activities, group work, and cultural exchange, participants developed their communication, teamwork, creativity, and digital skills.

  • Youth Exchange

    Plant Your Seeds of unity

    Ureki, Georgia

    The project focused on peacebuilding, tolerance, respect, inclusion, intercultural understanding, and active youth participation. Through debates, hikes, group activities, and intercultural exchange, participants had the opportunity to share perspectives, challenge stereotypes, and build meaningful connections across borders.

  • Youth Exchange

    Beyond the Game

    Ureki, Georgia

    The project went beyond sports and competition, focusing on inclusion, equality, teamwork, resilience, and intercultural understanding. Through sports activities, group challenges, and cultural exchange, participants developed new skills, strong social connections, and shared experiences with people from different backgrounds.

  • Youth Exchange

    Act4Peace

    Ureki, Georgia

    The project focused on peacebuilding, dialogue, intercultural understanding, and active youth participation. Through workshops, discussions, group activities, and intercultural exchange, participants explored ways young people can contribute to creating more inclusive, peaceful, and connected communities.

  • Youth Exchange

    Creative Recycle

    Ureki, Georgia

    Throughout the week, participants explored how art can become a powerful voice for the planet and a tool for raising awareness about environmental issues. They planted trees, created an eco-mural, visited the Dendrological Park and Botanical Garden, participated in clean-up activities, and discovered new ways to recycle and reuse materials.

  • Youth Exchange

    Be Positive, Be Strong

    Gonio, Georgia

    The exchange focused on teamwork, healthy lifestyles, physical activity, and mental well-being. Participants took part in sports activities, energizers, discussions, and outdoor experiences designed to encourage an active and healthy lifestyle.

  • Youth Exchange

    Proud of Me

    Kobuleti, Georgia

    The project focused on LGBTQ+ rights, equality, inclusion, acceptance, and respect. Participants came together as a diverse international group to explore the importance of creating a society where everyone can express their identity authentically and live without fear or discrimination.

  • Youth Exchange

    Across the Lines of Conflict

    Kobuleti, Georgia

    The project explored important topics including war, refugees, inclusion, conflict, empathy, and intercultural understanding. Participants from diverse backgrounds came together to learn more about the experiences and challenges faced by refugees, while discussing ways to promote inclusion and contribute to conflict resolution.

  • Youth Exchange

    CLICKBAIT: Unveiling the Truth!

    Gonio, Georgia

    The project explored the dark side of fake news, clickbait, and misinformation, helping participants understand how misleading information is created, spread, and used to influence audiences. Participants learned how to identify clickbait, evaluate information critically, recognize manipulative content, and become more responsible digital citizens.
