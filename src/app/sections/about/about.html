Our Background and Community Roots

The Georgian Youth Development Association (GYDA) is a non-governmental, non-profit youth organization based in the Gldani district of Tbilisi, an area with a historically significant population of Internally Displaced Persons from Abkhazia and South Ossetia. For nearly a decade, our organization has dedicated itself to supporting these young individuals, ensuring their seamless integration into society through access to free educational resources and European mobility projects. We work with a modern vision, adapting internationally certified methodologies to foster the interpersonal and social competences of our local youth while actively championing Georgia’s integration into the European Union.

Civic Engagement and Strategic Partnerships

Our impact is driven by hands-on community engagement and strategic institutional partnerships. We recently signed a memorandum with Grigol Robakidze University—making us only the second organization after DRONI to secure such an agreement—granting us access to university premises for diverse non-formal workshops and gatherings. We have also hosted significant diplomatic and civic events, including an interactive lecture in Bakuriani with Mr. Andrius Kalindra, the Ambassador of the Republic of Lithuania, focusing on the EU’s role in the South Caucasus and the importance of peaceful conflict resolution. Furthermore, our "Pieces of Peace" Karl Popper-style debate tournaments at the University of Georgia and the historic Peace Walk Georgia at the Mikheil Saakashvili Presidential Library have successfully united youths, politicians, and local citizens to advocate for international peace agreements.

Grassroots Charity and Environmental Action

Beyond civic education, GYDA is deeply committed to grassroots charity and environmental stewardship. We successfully collaborated with the "Merrily Masks" theatre to raise over one thousand GEL for stage decorations and costumes, an initiative that was featured in a short reportage by the Georgian Public Broadcaster, Pirveli Arkhi. Our team has also directly supported young people with disabilities, successfully collecting funds to provide new wheelchairs for local youth named Luka and Mariam. On the environmental front, our "Let’s keep our city Clean" campaign brought together members and volunteers to clear over five thousand square meters of territory around Tbilisi. Through meticulous planning, administrative management, and comprehensive monitoring of Erasmus+ and Youth in Action programs, our dedicated team continues to create meaningful, lasting impacts across the region.
